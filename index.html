      (0.25 × D) +

PyQGIS Automation Script (Report Page 26)

Automates vector clipping, 500m Gomti river buffer generation, and at-risk hospital extraction.

import processing
from qgis.core import QgsProject

# Set standard projected coordinate system for metric accuracy (EPSG:32644 UTM Zone 44N)
target_crs = "EPSG:32644"

# 1. Clip hospital points to Lucknow District administrative boundary
hospitals_clipped = processing.run("native:clip", {
    "INPUT": "data/hospitals.shp",
    "OVERLAY": "data/lucknow_district_boundary.shp",
    "OUTPUT": "memory:hospitals_clipped"
})["OUTPUT"]

# 2. Generate 500 m dissolved flood risk buffer around Gomti river
river_buffer = processing.run("native:buffer", {
    "INPUT": "data/gomti_river.shp",
    "DISTANCE": 500.0,
    "SEGMENTS": 10,
    "END_CAP_STYLE": 0,
    "JOIN_STYLE": 0,
    "DISSOLVE": True,
    "OUTPUT": "memory:river_500m_buffer"
})["OUTPUT"]

# 3. Extract hospitals intersecting the critical high-risk river buffer
at_risk_hospitals = processing.run("native:extractbylocation", {
    "INPUT": hospitals_clipped,
    "PREDICATE": [0], # 0 indicates 'intersect'
    "INTERSECT": river_buffer,
    "OUTPUT": "memory:at_risk_hospitals"
})["OUTPUT"]

# Print quantitative summary to console and add layer to active QGIS map canvas
print(f"Total Hospitals in High-Risk Buffer: {at_risk_hospitals.featureCount()}")
QgsProject.instance().addMapLayer(at_risk_hospitals)

7-Step Data Harmonization & Preparation Workflow (Page 20)

  1. Directory Organization: Structured dedicated folders for raw_data/, processed/, outputs/, and maps/.
  2. Reprojection: Standardized every vector and raster layer to WGS 84 / UTM Zone 44N (EPSG:32644).
  3. Clipping: Clipped all spatial layers strictly to the Lucknow District administrative boundary mask.
  4. Data Validation: Repaired invalid geometries with Fix Geometries; eliminated duplicate hospital/school nodes.
  5. Resampling & Alignment: Resampled all factor rasters to an identical 30 m grid cell alignment.

Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Heavy monsoon rainfall combined with low-elevation terrain regularly leads to severe waterlogging and flash flooding across low-lying localities in Lucknow District, Uttar Pradesh. During flooding events, municipal authorities and emergency planners require immediate spatial intelligence regarding which localities are most exposed, which healthcare centers and access roads are at risk of being cut off, and where displaced citizens can be sheltered safely.

By synthesizing five conditioning factors—ground elevation (30%), distance from major rivers (25%), monsoon precipitation (20%), land use land cover (15%), and terrain slope (10%)—through multi-criteria weighted overlay modeling in QGIS 3.28 LTR, the district was classified into Low (58%), Medium (27%), and High (15%) flood hazard zones. Furthermore, infrastructure exposure analysis revealed that 24 healthcare facilities and 168 km of arterial roadways lie inside the primary High Flood Risk corridor.

Recommendations for Disaster Management (Page 30)

  • Evacuation & Early Warning: Establish priority flood warning sirens and predetermined evacuation routes for wards in the High Risk zone along Gomti riverbank meanders.
  • Drainage De-silting: Carry out pre-monsoon desilting of municipal storm drains and secondary nallahs connecting into the Gomti River to avoid urban backflow.
  • Zoning Restrictions: Strictly enforce municipal zoning regulations to prohibit new hospital, school, or dense residential development in High Flood Risk zones.
  • Relief Readiness: Pre-designate identified safe school and public open grounds in Low Risk zones (Sites A, B, C, D) as official emergency relief shelter centers.
